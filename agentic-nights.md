# Global AI Nights

**Cold winter nights. Warm community conversations.**

Global AI Nights is the seasonal evening event for November 2026 through January 2027. Chapters around the world bring people together for accessible talks, demos, discussions, or hands-on sessions around AI. You decide the format that suits your audience; this guide gives you two ready-made options, activity ideas, a learning resource, a speaker kit, and the copy and hashtags to promote it. Each part has its own chapter, listed below.

## Key details

- **Season:** November 2026–January 2027
- **Format:** Evening meetup
- **Suggested duration:** 2–3 hours (about 3 hours for a hands-on Night)
- **Audience:** Local developers, AI practitioners, students, and community members
- **Content:** Use the shared seasonal content or add locally relevant sessions
- **Eligibility:** Organized by registered Global AI chapters

Use the [Organizer 101 guide](/organisers-101/) for practical planning support. Questions can be shared in [Discord](https://gaic.io/discord) or sent to [support@globalai.community](mailto:support@globalai.community).
