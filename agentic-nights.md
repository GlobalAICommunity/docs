# Global AI Nights

**Cold winter nights. Warm community conversations.**

Global AI Nights is the seasonal evening event for November 2026 through January 2027. Chapters around the world bring people together for accessible talks, demos, discussions, or hands-on sessions around AI. You decide the format that suits your audience; this guide gives you two ready-made options, activity ideas, a learning resource, a speaker kit, and the copy and hashtags to promote it.

## Key details

- **Season:** November 2026–January 2027
- **Format:** Evening meetup
- **Suggested duration:** 2–3 hours (about 3 hours for a hands-on Night)
- **Audience:** Local developers, AI practitioners, students, and community members
- **Content:** Use the shared seasonal content or add locally relevant sessions
- **Eligibility:** Organized by registered Global AI chapters

## Choose your format

Pick the option that fits your audience and venue. Both are suggestions; adapt the timings freely.

|  | Option A: Hands-on Night | Option B: Talks and community Night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Developer-heavy audiences | Mixed or beginner audiences |
| Core of the evening | Building an agent together with the Agent Framework 101 course | Talks, a demo, and a light group activity |
| Attendees need | Laptop, Python 3.10+, code editor, Global AI Community account | Nothing but curiosity |
| Length | About 3 hours | 2 to 3 hours |
| Course role | Main activity | Take-home resource |
| Venue needs | Tables, power sockets, reliable Wi-Fi | Seating, screen, projector |

## Option A: Hands-on Night

Attendees leave having built their first AI agent. Plan for about 3 hours.

| Time | Session | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18:00 | Doors open, networking | Drinks, check laptops are charged |
| 18:20 | Welcome and Global AI Nights intro | Use the intro slides from the template |
| 18:30 | Short talk: what agents are and why they matter | 20 minutes, keep it accessible |
| 18:50 | Setup check | Python 3.10+, editor, Global AI Community account, course started but AI access not yet activated |
| 19:00 | Build together: Agent Framework 101 | 90 minutes including a short break midway |
| 20:30 | Show-and-tell | Two or three attendees demo their assistant |
| 20:50 | Wrap-up, group photo, social post | Use #GlobalAINights |

Prepare in advance:

- Send the [setup email](#setup-email-for-option-a) 3 to 5 days before
- Recruit one helper per 10 to 15 attendees to walk the room
- Confirm who approves extra course time or tokens on the night
- Test the venue Wi-Fi and have a backup hotspot
- Run through the course yourself so you know the common sticking points

## Option B: Talks and community Night

A relaxed evening of talks, a demo, and conversation, with the course as a take-home next step.

| Time | Session | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18:00 | Doors open, networking | Drinks and introductions |
| 18:30 | Welcome and Global AI Nights intro | Use the intro slides from the template |
| 18:45 | Talk 1 | An accessible topic for a broad audience |
| 19:15 | Talk 2 or live demo | Something more hands-on or technical |
| 19:45 | Break |  |
| 20:00 | Group activity | Pick one from the activity ideas below |
| 20:45 | Open discussion | Questions, ideas, what people are working on |
| 21:00 | Wrap-up, group photo, social post | Close with the course slide and #GlobalAINights |

Short on time or speakers? Run a 2-hour version: welcome, one talk, one activity, wrap-up.

## Activity ideas

Each activity works with either format. Pick whatever suits your room.

| Activity | How it works | Time | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Build the event's own assistant | Attendees follow Agent Framework 101, which builds an assistant for a community event, and adapt it to your Global AI Night | 90 min (or start together, finish at home) | Laptops, Python |
| AI wishes for 2027 | Everyone writes one thing they hope AI does, or doesn't do, in the new year and sticks it on a wall. Post a photo with #GlobalAINights | 15 min | Sticky notes, a wall |
| Lightning demos | Attendees get 3 minutes each to show something they built or use every day | 20 to 30 min | Screen, a sign-up sheet |
| Debate table | Pick a provocative question, such as "Will agents replace apps?", split the room and let each side make its case | 20 to 30 min | A good question and a moderator |

The AI wishes wall is a nice one to run at every Night: it spans the new year and gives us great content to share across chapters.

## Learning resource: Agent Framework 101

[Agent Framework 101: Build your first agent](https://globalai.community/learn/agent-framework-101) is a free, 90-minute beginner course where attendees build a Python event assistant with Microsoft Agent Framework.

Over 5 modules, attendees connect to Learn Models, build an agent, add a tool, keep conversation context, and test their agent. Completing every module check and the final test earns 500 points and a certificate.

What organizers need to know:

- **Prerequisites:** basic Python, Python 3.10 or newer, a code editor, and a terminal. No previous agent experience needed.
- **AI credits are included.** Attendees get their own API key and token allowance, so nobody needs to bring a provider API key.
- **The timer starts on activation.** Activating AI access starts a 180-minute window that does not pause, even if the browser closes. Attendees should set up Python first and activate only when ready to build.
- **Extra access needs approval.** Attendees can request more time or tokens, but an organizer must approve it.
- **No repo to clone.** Attendees create each file from the course examples.

## Speaker kit

Use the official PowerPoint template and speaker card so every Global AI Night looks like part of one worldwide series.

**PowerPoint template.** [Download the Global AI Nights PowerPoint template](/global-ai-nights-template.pptx) and share it with all your speakers ahead of time. Please keep:

- The Global AI Nights intro slide at the start of the evening
- Your chapter logo and name in the marked space
- The closing slide with the hashtags and the Agent Framework 101 link

**Speaker card.** Fill in each speaker's name, photo, talk title, and your event date, then share it on your social channels and event page in the run-up to your Night. The speaker card download is still to be added.

Good moments to post:

- When you announce your event, with the speaker cards

## Organizer checklist

Work through this from about six weeks out.

**6 weeks before**

- Choose your format: Option A or Option B
- Book a venue and pick a date between November 2026 and January 2027
- Confirm speakers and share the PowerPoint template

**4 weeks before**

- Publish your event through the Global AI Community platform, using the event description
- Create and post speaker cards with #GlobalAINights
- Pick your activity and gather any materials

**1 week before**

- Send the setup email (Option A)
- Confirm helpers and who approves extra course access
- Check speaker slides use the template

**On the night**

- Test screen, Wi-Fi, and power
- Run the welcome with the intro slides
- Take photos, including the AI wishes wall and the group photo

**After the event**

- Post your recap with #GlobalAINights and tag the Global AI Community
- Share the course link with attendees who want to keep building
