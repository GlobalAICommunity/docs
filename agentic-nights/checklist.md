# Organizer checklist

Work through this from about six weeks out.

**6 weeks before**

- Choose your format: Option A or Option B
- Book a venue and pick a date between November 2026 and January 2027
- Confirm speakers and share the PowerPoint template

**4 weeks before**

- Publish your event through the Global AI Community platform, using the event description
- Create and post speaker cards with #GlobalAINights
- Pick your activity and gather any materials

**1 week before**

- Send the setup email (Option A)
- Confirm helpers and who approves extra course access
- Check speaker slides use the template

**On the night**

- Test screen, Wi-Fi, and power
- Run the welcome with the intro slides
- Take photos, including the AI wishes wall and the group photo

**After the event**

- Post your recap with #GlobalAINights and tag the Global AI Community
- Share the course link with attendees who want to keep building

Use the [Organizer 101 guide](/organisers-101/) for practical planning support. Questions can be shared in [Discord](https://gaic.io/discord) or sent to [support@globalai.community](mailto:support@globalai.community).
