# Formats and agenda

Two ready-made formats for your Global AI Night, with sample agendas and activities that work with either.

## Choose your format

Pick the option that fits your audience and venue. Both are suggestions; adapt the timings freely.

|  | Option A: Hands-on Night | Option B: Talks and community Night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Developer-heavy audiences | Mixed or beginner audiences |
| Core of the evening | Building an agent together with the Agent Framework 101 course | Talks, a demo, and a light group activity |
| Attendees need | Laptop, Python 3.10+, code editor, Global AI Community account | Nothing but curiosity |
| Length | About 3 hours | 2 to 3 hours |
| Course role | Main activity | Take-home resource |
| Venue needs | Tables, power sockets, reliable Wi-Fi | Seating, screen, projector |

## Option A: Hands-on Night

Attendees leave having built their first AI agent. Plan for about 3 hours.

| Time | Session | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18:00 | Doors open, networking | Drinks, check laptops are charged |
| 18:20 | Welcome and Global AI Nights intro | Use the intro slides from the template |
| 18:30 | Short talk: what agents are and why they matter | 20 minutes, keep it accessible |
| 18:50 | Setup check | Python 3.10+, editor, Global AI Community account, course started but AI access not yet activated |
| 19:00 | Build together: Agent Framework 101 | 90 minutes including a short break midway |
| 20:30 | Show-and-tell | Two or three attendees demo their assistant |
| 20:50 | Wrap-up, group photo, social post | Use #GlobalAINights |

Prepare in advance:

- Send the [setup email](/agentic-nights/promotion#setup-email-for-option-a) 3 to 5 days before
- Recruit one helper per 10 to 15 attendees to walk the room
- Confirm who approves extra course time or tokens on the night
- Test the venue Wi-Fi and have a backup hotspot
- Run through the course yourself so you know the common sticking points

## Option B: Talks and community Night

A relaxed evening of talks, a demo, and conversation, with the course as a take-home next step.

| Time | Session | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18:00 | Doors open, networking | Drinks and introductions |
| 18:30 | Welcome and Global AI Nights intro | Use the intro slides from the template |
| 18:45 | Talk 1 | An accessible topic for a broad audience |
| 19:15 | Talk 2 or live demo | Something more hands-on or technical |
| 19:45 | Break |  |
| 20:00 | Group activity | Pick one from the activity ideas below |
| 20:45 | Open discussion | Questions, ideas, what people are working on |
| 21:00 | Wrap-up, group photo, social post | Close with the course slide and #GlobalAINights |

Short on time or speakers? Run a 2-hour version: welcome, one talk, one activity, wrap-up.

## Activity ideas

Each activity works with either format. Pick whatever suits your room.

| Activity | How it works | Time | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Build the event's own assistant | Attendees follow Agent Framework 101, which builds an assistant for a community event, and adapt it to your Global AI Night | 90 min (or start together, finish at home) | Laptops, Python |
| AI wishes for 2027 | Everyone writes one thing they hope AI does, or doesn't do, in the new year and sticks it on a wall. Post a photo with #GlobalAINights | 15 min | Sticky notes, a wall |
| Lightning demos | Attendees get 3 minutes each to show something they built or use every day | 20 to 30 min | Screen, a sign-up sheet |
| Debate table | Pick a provocative question, such as "Will agents replace apps?", split the room and let each side make its case | 20 to 30 min | A good question and a moderator |

The AI wishes wall is a nice one to run at every Night: it spans the new year and gives us great content to share across chapters.
