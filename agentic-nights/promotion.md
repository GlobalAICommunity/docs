# Promotion and attendee emails

Hashtags, ready-to-use event copy, and the setup email for a Hands-on Night.

## Hashtags and social sharing

Use **#GlobalAINights** on every post, plus **#GlobalAICommunity**. You're welcome to add a city tag such as #GlobalAINightsAmsterdam.

Good moments to post:

- When you announce your event, with the speaker cards
- During the evening: talks, demos, people building
- The AI wishes for 2027 wall
- The group photo at the end

Tag the Global AI Community on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/global-ai-community) and [X](https://x.com/GlobAICommunity) so we can reshare your Night.

## Event description

Copy this into your event page and add your own agenda details to the second paragraph.

> **Global AI Nights**
> *Cold winter nights. Warm community conversations.*
>
> Join us for Global AI Nights, part of a worldwide series of evening events hosted by Global AI Community chapters from November 2026 through January 2027.
>
> This is an evening to come together, share what we've learned, and explore what's next in AI. Expect accessible talks, live demos, open discussion, and plenty of time to connect with others in the local AI community.
>
> No deep expertise required. Whether you're a developer, data scientist, student, business leader, or simply curious about AI, there's a seat for you. Bring your questions and ideas, and leave with new knowledge and new connections.
>
> Grab a warm drink and join the conversation. We look forward to seeing you there!

**Short version** for event cards and social posts:

> *Cold winter nights. Warm community conversations. Join us for Global AI Nights, part of a worldwide series of evening events hosted by Global AI Community chapters. Enjoy accessible talks, demos, and discussion with others curious about AI, and connect with your local community.*

## Setup email for Option A

Send this to registered attendees 3 to 5 days before a Hands-on Night.

> **Subject:** Get ready for Global AI Nights: build your first AI agent
>
> Hi there,
>
> We're looking forward to seeing you at Global AI Nights on [date] at [venue]! During the evening we'll build a first AI agent together using the free Agent Framework 101 course.
>
> To make the most of the time, please do this before you arrive:
>
> 1. Bring a laptop and charger.
> 2. Install Python 3.10 or newer and a code editor, such as VS Code.
> 3. Create a free account at globalai.community.
> 4. Open the course page: https://globalai.community/learn/agent-framework-101
>
> One important tip: don't activate the AI access in the course yet. It starts a 180-minute timer that can't be paused, so we'll activate it together on the night.
>
> No agent experience needed, just basic Python. See you there!
>
> [Your name]
> [Chapter name]
