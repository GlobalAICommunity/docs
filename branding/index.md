## Typography

Both fonts are available under the SIL Open Font License. The handbook self-hosts them so reading does not depend on an external font service.

</div>
<div class="landing-grid landing-grid-two">
<div class="landing-card brand-type brand-type-heading">

### Space Grotesk

Headings, eyebrows, statistics, and dates.

- **Display headline** · 650 · Large, confident, and short; scale down on small screens
- **Section heading** · 600 · Clear hierarchy without heavy decoration
- **Eyebrow** · 500 · Small uppercase label with letter spacing
- **Statistics and dates** · 600 · Use tabular figures when numbers align

[Get Space Grotesk](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk)

</div>
<div class="landing-card brand-type">
<p class="brand-type-sample">Aa</p>

### Instrument Sans

Body text and interface labels.

- **Body text** · 400 · Comfortable reading size and generous line spacing
- **UI labels** · 500–600 · Short, direct, and readable

[Get Instrument Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans)

## Layout, accessibility and voice

</div>
<div class="landing-grid">
<div class="landing-card">

### Layout and components

- Keep navigation and hero bands on cream; let main content live on white.
- Use rounded cards, generous spacing, and clear headings rather than excessive borders and decoration.
- Buttons are pill-shaped. Use one primary action per viewport and no more than two adjacent buttons.
- Use warm notices for date changes and important guidance. Explain what the reader should do.
- Finish pages with an ink footer and a useful next step.
- Use real community photography, not stock or AI-generated event photos.
- Keep motion restrained. Respect reduced-motion preferences and let moving strips pause on hover.

</div>
<div class="landing-card">

### Accessibility

Maintain at least **4.5:1** contrast for normal text and **3:1** for large text. Use deep orange rather than assuming the brighter brand orange works for small text on every background. Give keyboard focus a visible outline, label controls, and never communicate status through color alone.

</div>
<div class="landing-card">

### Voice

Write developer-to-developer: direct, friendly, and specific. Say what happens next.

- ~~View our upcoming events~~ → Find your next event
- ~~Access our comprehensive resources~~ → Find a workshop
- ~~Contact us for further assistance~~ → Email support

Use real, verified numbers with a date when the context requires one. Do not copy sample community statistics from a design mockup into current partner materials.

</div>
