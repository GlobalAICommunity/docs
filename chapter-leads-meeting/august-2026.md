**Global AI Chapter Insider: Advisory Board Nominations, a New Season, and a New Award**

## 1. Nominations Open: Join the Global AI Advisory Board

The Global AI Community has grown to more than **200 chapters** across **70 countries**. The Advisory Board gives chapter leads and long-time community members a formal voice in shaping how the community grows, from the event calendar and platform to partnerships and chapter support.

- **Seats available**: 5
- **Term**: 1 year, October 1 \u2013 October 1
- **Nominations close**: September 15, 2026
- **Who can nominate**: Active chapter leads and long-time community members

[Know more](/chapter-playbook/advisory-board.md) about the Advisory Board and how to nominate yourself or someone else.

## 3. A New Way to Manage Your Chapter

We've moved away from Umbraco, and everything you need is now in one place. The new Global AI platform lets you manage your chapter, events, and more through a single dashboard.

- **Chapter Dashboard**: See your member count, event stats, leads, co-organizers, and growth at a glance.
- **Event Check-in Scanner**: Use the built-in scanner to check attendees in at your events, no third-party apps needed.
- **Email Your Members**: Send messages directly to your chapter members straight from the platform.

Head over and take a look.

## 4. Introducing Global AI Learn

A new addition to the platform: **[Global AI Learn](https://globalai.community/learn)**, built to help members build practical AI skills. Work through focused courses, check your understanding, and earn a shareable badge when you complete the final test.

Courses are currently being prepared. Check back soon for the first Global AI Learn course.

## 5. Global AI is Ending Its Meetup Presence

We will be closing our Meetup groups as part of this transition. If you would like your group to be transferred to Azure Tech Groups rather than closed, please [send us an email](mailto:support@globalai.community?subject=Meetup%20Transfer) before **August 20** and we'll take care of it.
