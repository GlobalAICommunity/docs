# Run a chapter

<section class="landing-section">
<div class="landing-section-heading">

## New to chapter leadership?

Most chapters start the same way: a few people in one city who wanted somewhere to learn about AI, together. This is how that becomes a chapter.

</div>
<ol class="landing-steps">
<li>

### Find your people

Join [Discord](/chapter-playbook/discord), say hello in `#general`, and ask experienced chapter leads what worked for them. Find at least one co-organizer. Nobody runs a chapter alone.

</li>
<li>

### Apply

Check the [eligibility and responsibilities](/new-chapter), then send your application. HQ reviews new chapters once a month.

</li>
<li>

### Get set up

Once approved, you receive your chapter email and work through the [onboarding checklist](/chapter-playbook/onboarding#getting-set-up): Discord, your chapter dashboard, and your LinkedIn page.

</li>
</ol>
</section>

<div class="landing-section-heading">

## What do you need to do?

Your first event doesn't need to be big. It needs to happen, and then happen again. Chapters meet at least once every three months; many meet monthly.

</div>
<div class="landing-grid">
<div class="landing-card">

[Event Organization 101](/organisers-101/)

</div>
<div class="landing-card">

### Choose your programs

Join global moments such as Global AI Bootcamp, Global AI Community Connect, and Global AI Nights, or run your own meetups in between.

[Event calendar & formats](/event-format)

</div>
<div class="landing-card">

### Bring content and a look

Ready-made workshops and talks, presentation templates, and a logo for your city.

[Content & creative](/resources/)

<section class="landing-section landing-split">
<div>

## Keep your community welcoming

</div>
<div>

A chapter is only as good as the room feels. Events are free and open to everyone, and nothing is sold. Plan for accessibility from the start.

Make the [Code of Conduct](/chapter-playbook/code-of-conduct) visible to members and attendees, and act on it when it matters. These commitments are what make a chapter part of the community; see the [chapter requirements](/chapter-playbook/requirements).

</div>
</section>

## Changing your organizing team

Teams change. People move, get busy, or hand over to someone new. Tell [support@globalai.community](mailto:support@globalai.community) when organizers join or leave, or if you need to hand over or close your chapter.

::: details Proposed processes awaiting approval
The [transfer guide](/chapter-playbook/transferring), [offboarding guide](/chapter-playbook/offboarding), and escalation section of the [chapter requirements](/chapter-playbook/requirements) are drafts. They are available for review, but their proposed timelines and procedures are not approved policy.
:::

</section>
