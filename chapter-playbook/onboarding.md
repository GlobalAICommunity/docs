# Onboarding a New Chapter

Your chapter has been approved. Congratulations! Before your first event, take an hour to set up the basics. Doing this now means members can find you, other organizers can reach you, and your chapter looks like part of the Global AI Community from day one.

## Eligibility

Not approved yet? Start with [Start a Global AI Chapter](/new-chapter). It covers what a chapter is, the commitments you make, and [how to apply](/new-chapter#how-to-apply). In short: a local, city-based chapter, at least two organizers, free and not-for-profit events, and activity at least once every three months.

## Getting set up

Your welcome package includes your chapter's `@globalai.community` email address. Please don't buy domains or create email addresses yourself; HQ manages these centrally.

### Chapter email

Start using your chapter email right away. Use it to sign up for tools, request access, and talk to your members. It keeps the chapter's accounts with the chapter, even when organizers change.

### Discord

1. Join the [Global AI Discord server](https://discord.gg/NMryZKPC3m).
2. Email your Discord username to [support@globalai.community](mailto:support@globalai.community) and ask for access to the **#new-chapter-lead** channel.
3. Use the channel for announcements, updates, and organizer support.

The [Discord guide](/chapter-playbook/discord) explains roles and access.

### Chapter Dashboard

Log in to the [Chapter Dashboard](https://globalai.community/my/chapters), then:

- Add a short description of your chapter.
- Add all organizers, with profile photos and social links.

This is how people find your chapter and know who runs it.

### LinkedIn Showcase page

Email [support@globalai.community](mailto:support@globalai.community) to request a LinkedIn Showcase page for your chapter. Please don't create a separate company page. See the [LinkedIn guide](/chapter-playbook/linkedin).

### Events on the Global AI Community platform

Publish your events through the Global AI Community platform. HQ no longer pays for Meetup groups for chapters; read the [Meetup retirement notice](/chapter-benefits/meetup).

## Before your first event

Read [Event Organization 101](/organisers-101/) for planning your first meetup, and the [brand guidelines](/branding/) for your logo and visuals. Then publish your event and invite your first members.

## What's next

Once you're set up, read the [chapter requirements](/chapter-playbook/requirements). They explain what keeps a chapter active, welcoming, and part of the community.
