# Global AI Conference

The [Global AI Conference](https://globalaiconference.city/) is a free, one-day conference series for developers building with AI agents. Global AI HQ runs the series, selects the cities, and works with invited local chapter teams.

Dates, cities, registration, speaking opportunities, and sponsorship are all on the conference website.

::: info Previously AgentCon
The Global AI Conference continues the format previously known as AgentCon. Read the [AgentCon announcement](/agentcon/).
:::

## Attend or speak

Find your city, register for free, and check each city's call for speakers on [globalaiconference.city](https://globalaiconference.city/).

## Sponsor

Sponsorship packages and terms are on the [Global AI Conference sponsor page](https://globalaiconference.city/sponsor.html). For year-round partnerships, see [Partner with the Global AI Community](/partnerships/overview).

## Organize

Global AI Conference events are organized by invitation only. HQ contacts chapter leads when a city is planned for their region and shares organizer guidance directly with invited teams.

Want to host a conference with your chapter? Use the open, chapter-organized format: [Global AI Community Connect](/global-ai-conference/).
