CALL FOR HOSTS  /  2027

Bring Ignite
to your city.

Microsoft Ignite //localhost is a free, in-person event where you bring an official Ignite session to your local community. The best part comes after the demo, when someone opens their laptop and asks, “Could we try that on my project?”

Host for your Global AI chapter or as an individual between 11 January and 15 February 2027. You don’t need to be a chapter lead or a Microsoft MVP.

See how to apply  →

Apply by 30 December 2026.

WHO TO BRING TOGETHER

Start with a question your community keeps asking

Maybe students in your city want to see how an AI app goes from an idea to a working demo. Maybe local developers already have a prototype and want to compare what worked. Invite both. A first-time builder might ask the very question an experienced speaker forgot to explain.

You don’t have to cover the whole conference. Choose one approved Ignite topic, invite a local practitioner to share an example, and leave space for attendees to try it or talk it through. People who couldn’t travel to Ignite get to learn it together, close to home.

PLANNING YOUR IGNITE EVENT

What could you do in three hours?

Plan for three to four hours. Here’s one way to spend an evening together:

18:00 Welcome everyone, then kick off the Ignite breakout.
19:15 Try a hands-on lab if confirmed, or demo a local use case.
20:30 Open the floor for questions and introductions. Wrap by 21:00.

You don’t have to start with a blank slide deck

We’ll email approved hosts when the official session and lab materials are ready. You can request up to three optional labs. In the meantime, use the agenda and lab checklist to plan your evening. Microsoft’s host readiness sessions run from 4 to 15 January 2027.

FROM THE ORGANIZER DESK

Have a city, but no venue yet?

You can apply with your city and a proposed date while you look for a room. Confirm your venue by 8 January 2027.

On the event form, choose Ignite //Localhost under Global calendars. We’ll email you a decision within 48 hours of receiving your complete application.

What about costs and registration?

Your acceptance email sets your local limit, up to US$500 in eligible costs. Full reimbursement requires at least 30 in-person scanner check-ins. Once approved and your venue is confirmed, share your Global AI registration link anywhere, but don’t take sign-ups elsewhere: that makes the event ineligible for reimbursement. See what’s covered.

On the day, scan every attendee and collect feedback through Global AI. All hosts report by 28 February 2027, even if they don’t claim expenses.
