# Global AI Conference: Call for Speakers (CFS) Page

## Event Description

Global AI Conference is a one-day, community-driven developer conference focused on AI, built by developers, for developers. Hosted as part of the Global AI Community, the event brings together local developers, engineers, and AI enthusiasts for a day of hands-on learning, collaboration, and networking around AI frameworks, tools, and infrastructure.

The event features two tracks running in parallel: a **session track** with talks, demos, and discussions, and a **workshop track** with hands-on, practical sessions. Whether you're deploying AI in production, building agents, or exploring the latest frameworks, this is a space to share what you know and learn from others doing the same.

## Call for Speakers (CFS) Description

We're inviting speakers to submit session proposals for this year's Global AI Conference!

We're looking for talks, demos, panels, and hands-on workshops covering the full spectrum of AI, from foundational concepts to real-world production deployments. Topics of interest include (but aren't limited to):

- AI agents and agentic workflows
- Model Context Protocol (MCP)
- Generative AI and LLMs
- AI infrastructure and MLOps
- Responsible AI, ethics, and governance
- Developer tools and frameworks for AI
- Real-world case studies and lessons learned

Whether you're a first-time speaker or a seasoned presenter, we'd love to hear your story. Submit your session below!
