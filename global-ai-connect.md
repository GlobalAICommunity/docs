# Global AI Connect

**Connect with the community.**

Global AI Connect runs during May and June 2027. It gives chapters a flexible format for strengthening relationships between community members, speakers, organizers, students, professionals, and local partners.

## Key details

- **Season:** May–June 2027
- **Format:** Community meetup, networking event, panel, showcase, or unconference
- **Purpose:** Create meaningful connections across the local AI ecosystem
- **Content:** Locally shaped, with shared activity ideas from Global AI Community
- **Eligibility:** Organized by registered Global AI chapters

## Suggested formats

- Community networking evening.
- Local AI project showcase.
- Career and mentoring meetup.
- Panel connecting industry, education, and community.
- Chapter-to-chapter exchange or regional gathering.
- Open-space or unconference session.

## Organizer flow

1. Choose the people and communities you want to connect.
2. Select a format that encourages participation and conversation.
3. Publish the event on the Global AI Community platform.
4. Make space for introductions, community updates, and next steps.
5. Share new collaborations and outcomes after the event.

The seasonal organizer pack will include branding, activity ideas, and promotion guidance.
