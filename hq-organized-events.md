# HQ-organized events

Global AI HQ organizes a small number of strategic event programs. HQ defines the program, confirms locations, and invites chapter leads or local organizers to collaborate where needed.

These programs are separate from the seasonal chapter calendar and the year-round Global AI Community Connect conference format.

## Global AI Conference

The [Global AI Conference](https://globalaiconference.city/) is an invite-only, full-day conference series for developers building with AI agents. Global AI HQ selects locations and confirms each local co-organizing team. See the [Global AI Conference page](/conference) for how chapters get involved. It continues the format previously known as AgentCon; read the [AgentCon announcement](/agentcon/).

## Fly With Copilot

Fly With Copilot is an HQ-led event experience centered on practical learning and engagement with Copilot. Program details, locations, and participation opportunities will be announced by Global AI HQ.

## Future of Work Summit

Future of Work Summit brings together community, industry, and thought leaders to explore how AI is changing work. Global AI HQ coordinates the format, program, locations, and participating organizers.

## MCP Community Days

MCP Community Days is an HQ-led program focused on the Model Context Protocol and the community building with it. Event details and opportunities for local collaboration will be shared by Global AI HQ.

## How chapters participate

HQ will contact relevant chapter leads when an event is planned for their region. Being listed here does not mean a chapter can independently schedule or brand one of these events.
