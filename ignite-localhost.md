## The event format

### One Ignite experience. Your local community.

Choose sessions that answer your audience's questions. The [sample evening agenda](/ignite-localhost/content-and-labs.md#build-a-local-agenda) shows how to fit a breakout, a lab and discussion into three hours.

A strong &#47;&#47;localhost event can combine:

- A welcome and introduction to Microsoft Ignite.
- Curated breakout sessions, delivered live or facilitated locally.
- A hands-on lab, guided demo, or technical workshop.
- Local expert perspectives and community Q&A.
- Time for networking and meaningful connections.

Plan for a **three-to-four-hour** event. Breakout sessions are approximately **45 minutes** and instructor-led labs approximately **75 minutes**. A lab is not required, but including at least one is strongly encouraged. Approved hosts may request up to three labs.

</section>

## From application to reporting.

</div>
<ol class="landing-steps">
<li>

### Create your event

[Create and submit your event](/ignite-localhost/create-event.md) on the Global AI Community platform by 30 December 2026. Select **Ignite &#47;&#47;Localhost** under **Global calendars**.

</li>
<li>

### Build the local plan

You can submit before securing a venue, but the event remains pending until venue details are confirmed. Confirm the venue by 8 January, select the most relevant Ignite content, and shape the agenda.

Keep your Global AI Community event page up to date. Once your event is approved and the venue is confirmed, share that page with attendees. It must be the only place they can register. If another system accepts registrations, **we will decline reimbursement for the entire event**.

</li>
<li>

### Host and share

Use the Global AI Community scanner for every check-in and the Global AI Community platform for attendee feedback. Ask permission before taking or sharing photos. Every host must [submit an event report](/ignite-localhost/reporting.md) by **28 February 2027**, even without a reimbursement claim.

</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="landing-section landing-split">
<div>

## Keep the experience local.

</div>
<div>

The official Ignite content is the starting point, not the whole event. Choose material that fits your audience and make room for local context, practical questions, and hands-on learning while preserving the approved content and product claims.

Follow the [Global AI Community Code of Conduct](/chapter-playbook/code-of-conduct.html) and avoid scheduling conflicts with major local cultural or religious events. Choose a venue with accessible entry and seating, reliable Wi-Fi, display or projection, audio, power, check-in space, and room for networking. For labs, make sure attendees can use their laptops comfortably.

</div>
</section>

<section class="landing-section">
<div class="landing-section-heading">

## Content and lab preparation.

Choose approved topics that fit your audience, then prepare the people, venue, and backup plan needed to deliver them well.

</div>
<div class="landing-grid landing-grid-two">
<div class="landing-card">

### Keep official content accurate

Add local examples, context, and discussion without changing Microsoft product claims, removing required messaging, or presenting modified material as official Microsoft content.

</div>
<div class="landing-card">

### Test every lab

Review the prerequisites and complete a full lab dry run at least one day before the event. Test Wi-Fi, AV, power, and the attendee experience.

</div>
<div class="landing-card">

### No attendee Azure credits required

The supplied lab environments do not require attendees to provide Azure credits or already have a Microsoft or customer account.

</div>
<div class="landing-card">

### Prepare a backup agenda

Have an alternative approved session, demo, or discussion ready in case a lab or speaker becomes unavailable.

</div>
