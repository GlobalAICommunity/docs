# Create your Ignite &#47;&#47;localhost event

Submit your **Microsoft Ignite &#47;&#47;localhost 2027** event through Global AI Community by **30 December 2026**. You can apply with just your city if you do not have a confirmed venue yet.

<p><a class="button button-ink" href="https://globalai.community/my/events/add?kind=global">Create your event <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a></p>

## Step 1: Sign in or create an account

Go to [globalai.community](https://globalai.community/) and sign in.

If you do not have an account yet, create a free account first. Anyone can create an account and organize an Ignite &#47;&#47;localhost event through the Global AI Community.

## Step 2: Open Events and organize an event

From your account navigation:

1. Select **Events**.
2. Select **Organize an event**.

Do not select **Add an external event**. That option is for listing an event that is organized and managed somewhere else.

You can also open the [event creation form directly](https://globalai.community/my/events/add?kind=global).

<figure class="event-guide-image">
<img src="/media/ignite-localhost/02-organize-event.png" alt="Global AI Community Events page with the Events navigation selected and the Organize an event button visible">
<figcaption>Open <strong>Events</strong>, then select <strong>Organize an event</strong>.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Step 3: Choose who is organizing

Choose the organizer identity that applies to your event:

- **As your chapter:** Select your Global AI chapter when you are a chapter lead or have permission to organize on its behalf.
- **As a person:** Select this when you are not organizing through a chapter and want to host the event independently.

The choices shown depend on the chapters and organizer permissions connected to your account.

<figure class="event-guide-image">
<img src="/media/ignite-localhost/03-choose-organizer.png" alt="Global AI event form asking who is organizing, with options to organize as a person or on behalf of a chapter">
<figcaption>Choose your chapter when organizing on its behalf; otherwise choose <strong>As a person</strong>.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Step 4: Select the Ignite &#47;&#47;Localhost calendar

Under **Global calendars**, select **Ignite &#47;&#47;Localhost**.

Do not select **General event**. Selecting the Ignite calendar connects your event to the program and its approval, content, support, and reimbursement process.

<figure class="event-guide-image">
<img src="/media/ignite-localhost/04-select-calendar.png" alt="Global AI event type screen showing General event and Ignite Localhost under Global calendars">
<figcaption>Under <strong>Global calendars</strong>, select <strong>Ignite &#47;&#47;Localhost</strong>.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Step 5: Set the date and time

Choose:

- An event date within the **11 January–15 February 2027** hosting window.
- The local start and end time.
- The correct timezone for the event location.

Choose the time zone for **your venue**, not the one in the example screenshot. This setting controls the times on your public event page.

::: warning Date restriction shown in the form
The screenshot shows a shorter date range than the program allows. You can host from **11 January through 15 February 2027**, inclusive. If the form blocks a date in that window, [contact organizer support](./organizer-support.md). Do not enter a different date just to submit the form.
:::

<figure class="event-guide-image">
<img src="/media/ignite-localhost/05-when.png" alt="Global AI event form fields for date, start time, end time, and timezone">
<figcaption>Choose the event date, local start and end time, and the correct timezone.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Step 6: Select the location

Ignite &#47;&#47;localhost is an **in-person** event, so the event type is already set to **In-person**.

If you know the venue:

1. Search for the venue, street, city, or postcode.
2. Select the correct result and check the map marker.
3. Confirm the venue name, address, city, and country.

<figure class="event-guide-image">
<img src="/media/ignite-localhost/06-location-search.png" alt="Global AI event form location search and map for an in-person event">
<figcaption>Search for the venue or location and check the map marker.</figcaption>
</figure>

If you do not know the venue yet:

1. Enter the event **city**.
2. Use **TBA** for the venue name and address.
3. Select the correct country.

You must confirm the final venue details by **8 January 2027**.

<figure class="event-guide-image">
<img src="/media/ignite-localhost/06-location-details.png" alt="Global AI event form with TBA venue details, a city, and a selected country">
<figcaption>If the venue is not confirmed, enter the city and use <strong>TBA</strong> for the venue name and address.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Step 7: Add the event details

The event name is generated automatically using the format `Ignite //Localhost - {city}`.

Add a clear description that tells attendees:

- Who the event is for.
- What they will learn.
- That the event is in person and free to attend.
- The expected format, such as breakout sessions, a lab, Q&A, and networking.

You can refine the description and agenda after the event has been approved.

<figure class="event-guide-image">
<img src="/media/ignite-localhost/07-details.png" alt="Global AI event details screen with an automatically generated Ignite Localhost event name and a description field">
<figcaption>Check the automatically generated event name and add the event description.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Step 8: Review and create the event

Review every detail before creating the event:

- Organizer and Ignite &#47;&#47;Localhost calendar.
- Event name and description.
- Registration on your public Global AI Community event page only.
- Date, local time, and timezone.
- In-person format.
- Venue or TBA location details.

Pay particular attention to the timezone and city. If anything is wrong, go back and correct it before selecting **Create event**.

<figure class="event-guide-image">
<img src="/media/ignite-localhost/08-confirm.png" alt="Global AI event confirmation screen summarizing the event type, name, registration, date, format, location, and description">
<figcaption>Review the complete summary, then select <strong>Create event</strong>.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Step 9: Wait for review

After you submit your event, the dashboard shows **Under review**. We'll email you a decision **within 48 hours of receiving a complete application**. If your venue is still listed as **TBA**, your application stays pending. If editing is locked, [send HQ the confirmed venue details](./organizer-support.md) by **8 January 2027**.

While the event is under review:

- You cannot edit the event or manage registrations.
- You can review the submitted date, time, timezone, and location.
- The event-process panel shows the remaining approval, reporting, and reimbursement stages.

<figure class="event-guide-image">
<img src="/media/ignite-localhost/09-under-review.png" alt="Global AI event dashboard showing an Ignite Localhost event under review and the event process stages">
<figcaption>The example dashboard shows an older review estimate and claim date. Allow <strong>48 hours</strong> for a complete application. The last day to submit the report and any receipts is <strong>28 February 2027</strong>, not 1 March.</figcaption>
</figure>

After approval, check your acceptance details for your [approved budget](./reimbursement.md). Once the venue is confirmed, select **View public page** and share that link, not your dashboard. This must be the only place attendees register. Use the [host readiness checklist](./host-readiness.md) to prepare and the [reporting checklist](./reporting.md) after the event.
