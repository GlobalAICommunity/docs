# Digital assets

## Which files should I use?

For the **organizer logo**, [download the Global AI Community logos (ZIP)](/logos/gaic/global-ai-community-logos.zip). The [brand guide](/branding/) shows which version to use on light and dark backgrounds. These are community logos, not Microsoft Ignite campaign artwork.

::: info Coming soon: Ignite event artwork
We'll post the artwork here when it's ready. For session slides and labs, see [official content and labs](./content-and-labs.md).
:::

Use only approved artwork, slides and campaign messages for this edition. Do not reuse artwork from another event, redraw Microsoft logos or describe Microsoft as your sponsor.

## What to put in an announcement

Use the event name from your dashboard. Include the city, confirmed venue, date, local time and what attendees can expect. If you have a confirmed lab, say what to bring. Put these details in the text, not only in an image.

You can promote the event on other sites, but always link back to your **public Global AI Community event page**. Do not collect sign-ups on the site where you post the announcement.

**Example to adapt:**

> Join us in **[city]** on **[date]** at **[local time]** for a free, in-person Microsoft Ignite &#47;&#47;localhost event at **[venue]**. We'll explore **[confirmed topic]** and leave time for questions. Register here: **[link to your Global AI Community event page]**.

You can draft the announcement in advance. Share it after your event is approved and your venue is confirmed.
