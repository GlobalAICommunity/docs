## Applying to host

::: details Do I need to be a chapter lead or a Microsoft MVP?
No. Anyone can apply through Global AI Community. Choose your chapter if you organize on its behalf. Otherwise choose **As a person**.
:::

::: details How do I apply, and by when?
Submit by **30 December 2026**. Sign in to Global AI Community, open **Events**, select **Organize an event** and choose the **Ignite &#47;&#47;Localhost** calendar. Do not choose **Add an external event**. The [creation guide](./create-event.md) shows each screen.
:::

::: details Can I apply before I have a venue?
Yes. Enter your city and use **TBA** (“to be announced”) for the venue name and address. Your application stays pending until you confirm the venue. Confirm it by **8 January 2027**. If you cannot edit the event while it is under review, [contact HQ](./organizer-support.md).
:::

::: details How will I know if my event is approved?
We'll email you the approval decision **within 48 hours** for a complete application. While you wait, your dashboard shows **Under review**.
:::

::: details Which dates can I choose?
Host your event between **11 January and 15 February 2027**, including both dates. If the form blocks a valid date, ask HQ for help rather than choosing an incorrect date.
:::

## Planning and promoting the event

::: details Can I host online, run a hybrid event or charge for tickets?
No. This edition is **fully in person and free to attend**. Choose an accessible venue with room for your audience. Aim for 30 in-person check-ins to qualify for full reimbursement.
:::

::: details When can I invite attendees, and which link do I share?
Once your event is approved and your venue is confirmed, share your public **Global AI Community event page**. You can share this link on other sites, but if another system accepts registrations for your event, **we will decline reimbursement for the entire event**. See [digital assets](./digital-assets.md) for help preparing your announcement.
:::

::: details How long should the event be? Is a lab required?
Plan for **three to four hours**. A breakout takes about 45 minutes; a lab takes about 75 minutes.

Labs are encouraged, not required. Approved hosts can request up to three. Attendees do not need their own Azure credits or an existing account for the supplied lab environments. See the [sample agenda and lab checklist](./content-and-labs.md).
:::

::: details Can I add my own examples?
Yes. Use approved Ignite material as the starting point. Add local examples, questions and discussion. Do not change Microsoft product claims or present your own slides as official Microsoft material.
:::

::: details What if I miss a host readiness session?
The sessions run from **4–15 January 2027**. We'll post the [session recordings on the host readiness page](./host-readiness.md#session-recordings) as they become available.
:::

## Money and reporting

::: details Does every approved host get US$500?
No. **US$500 is the program maximum**, not a grant or an advance. Your acceptance details give your own limit and approved expenses. You pay the vendor first and claim eligible costs afterward. Read the [reimbursement guide](./reimbursement.md) before booking or ordering.
:::

::: details Which costs can I claim?
You can claim venue costs as long as your total claim stays within the amount approved for your event. You can also claim food and non-alcoholic drinks if they are approved for your event. Alcohol, travel, lodging, and change or cancellation fees are not covered. If in doubt, ask HQ before you spend.
:::

::: details Can I claim from both Microsoft and Global AI Community?
No. Choose one organization for the whole event. If you submit to both, even for different expenses, **you get no reimbursement for the event**. If you claimed through Microsoft, still submit your Global AI event report without requesting payment.
:::

::: details Which receipts do I need?
Upload readable copies of the vendor's original itemized receipts in the event's **local currency**. Keep the originals. A fake receipt, an AI-generated receipt, or prices that do not match the local currency will disqualify the **whole event claim**. See the [reimbursement guide](./reimbursement.md).
:::

::: details What if 30 people sign up but only 25 come?
Report **30 registered and 25 attended**. You must use the Global AI Community check-in scanner for every attendee. The in-person check-in total, not registrations, counts toward the 30-person threshold for full reimbursement. Still submit your report. If you are claiming through Global AI Community, include your receipts. HQ may approve a smaller payment; there is no fixed amount for this case.
:::

::: details What do I submit, and when?
Every host submits an [event report](./reporting.md) by **28 February 2027** (before 1 March). Include separate registration and check-in totals, a short summary, feedback collected through Global AI Community, and event photos you have permission to share. If you are claiming expenses through Global AI Community, also include copies of the original itemized receipts and photos of your purchases. See the [reimbursement checklist](./reimbursement.md#reimbursement-checklist).
:::

::: details Who pays me, and how?
Global AI Community pays through **PayPal or Wise only**. If the first transfer fails, we try once more: **two attempts in total**. Check your details before submitting. If you use PayPal, make sure your account is set up to receive money.
:::
