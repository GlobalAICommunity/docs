# Host readiness

Microsoft's host readiness sessions are scheduled for **4–15 January 2027**.

## Session recordings

Can't attend live? You'll find the recordings here after the sessions, as they become available.

## Before the session

- **Confirm your venue by 8 January 2027**, even if your readiness session is later. Check accessible entry and seating, room capacity, Wi-Fi, power, sound and a screen.
- Bring your proposed agenda and the topics your audience wants to cover. Note which sessions or labs you still need.
- Read your acceptance details. Check your budget and write down questions about expenses, registration or materials.

## Before you open registration

Once your event is approved and your venue is confirmed, share your public **Global AI Community event page**. This is where attendees register. Check that the page gives the local date and time, venue, who the event is for, and what to bring. The event must be **free and fully in person**.

## Before people arrive

- **At the door:** Assign someone to check in every attendee with the Global AI Community scanner. Have the [Code of Conduct](/chapter-playbook/code-of-conduct.md) ready to share.
- **In the room:** Name a host for each session. If you are running a lab, [test it and choose a backup activity](./content-and-labs.md) at least a day in advance.
- **For the report:** Ask attendees to give feedback through Global AI Community. Assign someone to ask permission for photos and [submit the event report](./reporting.md).
