# Organizer support

For help with a Global AI Community Ignite &#47;&#47;localhost event, email [support@globalai.community](mailto:support@globalai.community?subject=Ignite%20localhost%20support). You can ask as a chapter organizer or as an individual host.

| If this happens | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The form will not accept your date | Check that it is between **11 January and 15 February 2027** and that the timezone is right. If it still fails, contact support. Do not enter a false date. |
| Your event is **Under review** | We'll email you the approval decision **within 48 hours** for a complete application. You can view the event in your dashboard, but you cannot edit it or manage registrations yet. |
| Your venue is still listed as **TBA** | Confirm your venue by **8 January 2027**. Update the venue name and address in your dashboard, or send them to HQ by that date if editing is locked. |
| You are waiting for session or lab materials | When we receive them, we'll post them on the [content and labs page](./content-and-labs.md) and email them to approved hosts. You do not need to request the materials. |
| You need the attendee registration link | Once approved and the venue is confirmed, select **View public page** in your dashboard. Share that page, not the dashboard. Taking registrations in another system disqualifies the event from reimbursement. |
| You need to cancel or reschedule | Email [support@globalai.community](mailto:support@globalai.community) before announcing the change. If the new date falls outside **11 January–15 February 2027**, it needs a program exception. Wait for confirmation before sharing that date. |
| You do not know whether a purchase is covered | Check [reimbursement](./reimbursement.md) and ask HQ before spending. Include the item, amount and currency. |
| You cannot submit your report or upload receipts | [Check your deadline](./reporting.md) and contact HQ before it passes. Say which step failed. |

## What to include in your message

Give the event name, city, date and event link if you have one. Explain what you tried, what happened, and any deadline you are facing. For a platform issue, include the exact error or a screenshot with personal details hidden. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or attendee lists.

For questions about who can host, attendance, labs or timelines, start with the [host FAQ](./faq.md).
