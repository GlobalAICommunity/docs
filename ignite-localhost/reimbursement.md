# Organizer reimbursement

**You pay the vendor first.** After the event, you can claim approved costs up to the limit in your acceptance details. Your limit depends on your city and country; it is not a flat payment.

<dl class="claim-facts" aria-label="Reimbursement at a glance">
<div><dt>Program maximum</dt><dd>Up to US$500</dd></div>
<div><dt>For full reimbursement</dt><dd>30+ in person</dd></div>
<div><dt>Report and receipts</dt><dd>Before 1 March</dd><dd class="claim-cutoff">Last day to submit: 28 February 2027</dd></div>
</dl>

::: warning When an event gets no reimbursement
Any of the following disqualifies the **whole event claim**, not just one expense:

- **Taking registrations outside Global AI Community.** You can promote the event on other sites, but all sign-ups must go through your public Global AI Community event page.
- **Claiming from both Microsoft and Global AI Community.** Choose one for the entire event, even if you have different receipts for different expenses.
- **Submitting a fake or AI-generated receipt, or prices that do not match the local currency.** Submit original, itemized vendor receipts showing what you actually paid in the event's local currency. Do not convert prices to US dollars to match the program limit.
:::

## Reimbursement checklist

- **Original receipts:** Keep each vendor's itemized receipt in local currency. Check that your copies are readable and your total is within your approved budget.
- **Photos of purchases:** Photograph the items you bought for the event, such as food or drinks, and keep each photo with its receipt.
- **Event photos:** Take photos that show the size of the gathering. Ask permission before taking or sharing photos of people.
- **Attendance figures:** Use the Global AI Community check-in scanner for every attendee. Report registrations and check-ins separately. Use the scanner total, not a headcount from a photo.
- **Payment details:** Check that your PayPal or Wise account can receive money and that your payment details are correct.
- **Event report:** Include a short summary and the feedback attendees gave through the Global AI Community platform. Submit the [report](./reporting.md) and your claim by **28 February 2027**. Claim from **one organization only**.

## Before you spend

Read your acceptance details before you book a room or order food. Check **both** the amount and what it covers. If either is missing, [ask Global AI Community HQ](./organizer-support.md) before you agree to pay a vendor.

| Cost | Can I claim it? |
| --- | --- |
| Food and non-alcoholic drinks for the event | Yes, if these costs are approved for your event. |
| Venue expenses | Yes, as long as your total claim stays within the amount approved for your event. |
| Alcohol, travel or lodging | No. Leave these out of your claim. |
| Fees for changing or cancelling a booking | No. Check the vendor's terms before you book. |
| A cost not listed here | Ask HQ **before** spending. Do not assume it is covered. |

::: details Example: how much can I claim with a US$300 limit?
For an event in the United States with an approved limit of **US$300**:

- Spend US$240 on approved items → claim **US$240**.
- Spend US$350 on approved items → claim **US$300**.

Both examples assume you meet the attendance and reporting requirements.
:::

## On the day

- **Use the Global AI Community check-in scanner for every attendee as they arrive.** Use the scanner total for in-person attendance, not the number of sign-ups. You need at least **30 in-person attendees** to qualify for full reimbursement. If 40 people register and 27 check in, report 40 registrations and 27 attendees.
- **If fewer than 30 come, still submit your report.** If you are claiming through Global AI Community, include your receipts. HQ reviews smaller events individually. A reduced payment may be possible, but there is no guaranteed amount.
- **Keep the original itemized receipts.** An itemized receipt lists what you bought and what each item cost. Ask the vendor for one when you pay. If a receipt includes both food and alcohol, keep the receipt but claim only the approved food.

## After the event: submit the claim

1. Open your event dashboard on the Global AI Community platform and complete the [event report](./reporting.md). Give the registration total and the actual in-person count separately. If you claimed through Microsoft, submit the report **without requesting payment** from Global AI Community.
2. If you are claiming through Global AI Community, upload readable copies of the original itemized receipts in local currency **and photos of the items you bought**. Claim only eligible expenses within your approved limit. Keep the original receipts.
3. Submit your report and receipts after your event and **before 1 March 2027**. For all Global AI Community events in this program, the last day to submit is **28 February 2027**.

## How you get paid

::: important Up to two payment attempts
Before you submit your payment details:

1. **Choose PayPal or Wise.** Global AI Community does not pay through any other platform.
2. **Check your account details.** If you use PayPal, sign in and confirm the account is set up to **receive money**, not just send it.

If the first transfer fails, we try once more. We do not make a third attempt.
:::

There is no confirmed payment date in this guide. If you need a payment update or cannot upload a receipt, [contact HQ](./organizer-support.md) with your event link and date.
