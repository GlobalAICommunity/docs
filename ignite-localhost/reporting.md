# Event reporting

**Every host submits an event report**, even if fewer than 30 people attended or you are not asking for reimbursement. Submit it in your Global AI Community event dashboard after the event. **The last day is 28 February 2027** (before 1 March).

## What to collect

| What | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Registrations | Record the sign-up total from your **public Global AI Community event page**. If another system accepts registrations, the whole event is ineligible for reimbursement. |
| In-person attendance | Use the **Global AI Community check-in scanner** for every attendee. Report the scanner total, not the number who signed up. |
| Event summary | Say where and when you met, which sessions or labs you ran, and what attendees did. Note any changes to the plan. |
| Feedback | Ask attendees to give feedback through the **Global AI Community platform**. Summarize what they found useful and what you would change. |
| Event photos | Include photos of the event and attendees that show the size of the gathering. Ask permission before taking or sharing photos of people. Photos do not replace scanner check-ins. |

### Only if you claim expenses through Global AI Community

For each expense, upload a readable copy of the vendor's **original itemized receipt in local currency** and keep the original. Include photos of the items you bought, such as food or drinks. A photo of a purchase does not replace its receipt. Check your total against your [approved budget and reimbursement rules](./reimbursement.md).

## Submit your report

1. Open **Event Report** in your Global AI Community event dashboard.
2. Enter registrations and in-person check-ins as two separate numbers. Add your summary, feedback and event photos.
3. If you are claiming through Global AI Community, attach your expense evidence. If you chose Microsoft or are not claiming, **submit the report without requesting payment**. Submitting claims to both organizations disqualifies the whole event.

If you cannot submit, [contact organizer support](./organizer-support.md) before the deadline and explain what is blocking you.
