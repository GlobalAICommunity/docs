# Tools & resources

Spend less time starting from scratch. Find the tools and reusable materials that help you run a chapter and deliver a useful event.

## Get organizer tools

| What you need | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Azure subscriptions for a workshop | [Request Azure credits](/chapter-benefits/azure) at least 7 days before your event |
| Help attendees activate their subscription | [Azure activation walkthrough](/chapter-benefits/azure-client) |
| Canva for chapter graphics | [Canva access and eligibility](/chapter-benefits/canva) |
| Livestreaming support | [StreamYard guide](/streamyard), including instructions that need confirmation |
| A chapter LinkedIn presence | [Request a Showcase page](/chapter-playbook/linkedin) |

The [benefits overview](/chapter-benefits/) explains how to use these resources. Availability and eligibility are described on each benefit page; do not assume that a previous event's offer applies to a new program.

## Find something to teach

- [Workshops](/workshops): hands-on materials with duration, level, and prerequisites.
- [Presentations](/presentations): slide decks and repositories you can use to shape your sessions.
- [AgentCamp 2026 content](/agentcamp/content): archived workshops and talks that may still be useful.
- [Build //localhost 2026 content](/build/content): archived labs and breakout sessions.

Check the source repository and try the exercises before your event. Historical materials can be reusable without their old funding, registration, or support offers still being available.

## Create and promote

- [Branding assets and guidelines](/branding/): official logos, typography, colors, and visual principles.
- [Event photography checklist](/event-photography-checklist): capture the event's speakers, people, and atmosphere.
- [Global AI Conference promotion](/global-ai-conference/marketing-branding): planning and branding for the open conference format.

## Manage your chapter and events

Open your [chapter dashboard](https://globalai.community/my/chapters) to manage your chapter. Use [Event Organization 101](/organisers-101/) for the planning and publishing overview.

A detailed event-management walkthrough is still being prepared. For help with registrations, check-in, attendee communication, or event reports, contact [support@globalai.community](mailto:support@globalai.community).
